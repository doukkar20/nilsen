Construction Tools – Privacy Policy

Effective date: {30/12/2025 }

About This App

This app provides general company contact information and practical offline construction tools and calculators.

Developer: Nordic Build Tools

Package: dk.bentnielsen.constructiontools

Data Collection

No personal or sensitive data is collected, stored, or shared.

  • No account creation or login.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No ads.
  • No background services.

Device Permissions

The app does not request runtime permissions (location, storage, contacts, camera, microphone, etc.).

Contact Actions

Call, email, and maps buttons open the system dialer, email app, or maps app. The app does not capture or store any input from these actions.

Offline Tools

All calculators operate on-device and offline. Inputs stay on the device and are not transmitted or saved.

Results are approximate and for informational use only.

Third-Party Services

No third-party SDKs are integrated beyond AndroidX and Material Components provided by Google.

Children

This app is intended for general audiences and does not target children.

Changes

Any updates to this policy will be reflected in a future app release with an updated effective date.
